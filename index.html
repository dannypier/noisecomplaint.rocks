The Band

Four guys with day jobs and a shared appreciation for the loud part of the second chorus.

Noise Complaint is a four-piece rock cover band in Denver, Colorado. We play across the whole rock family — classic rock, punk, alternative and pop — and the thread running through all of it is that you already know the words. Nirvana, Green Day, Sublime, Blink-182, Radiohead, Olivia Rodrigo, Chappell Roan. No twenty-minute jams, no deep cuts nobody asked for. Just the choruses a room sings back at you.

We play taprooms, breweries, bars, backyards, block parties and private events around the Denver metro and the Front Range. Sets run anywhere from forty-five minutes to three hours, we bring our own gear, and we load out fast.
